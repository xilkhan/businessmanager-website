BusinessManager

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BusinessManager handles information when businesses use the BusinessManager desktop application.

1. Information stored by BusinessManager

BusinessManager is designed to store business information such as products, stock quantities, sales, purchases, customer details, supplier details, invoices and payment records.

This information is primarily stored in a PostgreSQL database on the computer where BusinessManager is installed.

2. Google Drive access

Businesses may optionally connect their own Google account to BusinessManager.

When a business connects Google Drive, BusinessManager uses Google's authorization process to obtain permission to create and manage files associated with the application.

BusinessManager requests the Google Drive file scope needed for application-managed files. It does not request the user's Google password.

3. Cloud synchronization

When Google Drive synchronization is connected and enabled, BusinessManager can upload application synchronization records to the connected business Google Drive account.

These records may contain business transaction information needed to synchronize changes. They are not a substitute for a complete database backup.

4. Google authorization information

BusinessManager stores Google authorization information locally using Windows data protection mechanisms. This information is used to maintain the authorized connection without asking the user to provide their Google password.

5. Data sharing

BusinessManager does not sell business records or customer information to advertisers. Information uploaded through Google Drive synchronization is stored in the Google account connected by the business.

6. Data security

BusinessManager uses account authentication, access permissions and protected local authorization information to help safeguard business records.

Businesses remain responsible for securing their computers, Windows accounts, Google accounts and access to their business data.

7. Data retention and deletion

Business records remain in the local business database until they are deleted by an authorized user or through an appropriate database removal process.

Files uploaded to Google Drive may remain in that Google account until deleted by an authorized account holder.

8. Contact
